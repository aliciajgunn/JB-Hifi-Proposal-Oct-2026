Why it works: the AI harness

An AI model can reason, but it can't guarantee. The harness is the layer around the model that turns reasoning into reliable action, built on the data and rules already in your Salesforce environment.

Understand

Your customers, orders, quotes, cases and products, so the agent never starts from zero.

Act

Look up an order, raise a case, draft a quote, using the workflows your team already runs.

Control

Your permissions, approvals and guardrails apply to every action, with a person in the loop where you want one.

A simple example. Asked what discount could win a deal, an AI model might suggest 18%. The business rules in the core system say the maximum a rep can offer is 15%. Models show what's probable. Your systems decide what's allowed. The harness connects the two.

JB Service Agent

Live in production

Answers business customers' questions on the Help & Support page, at any hour.

The problem it solves

Routine order, account and "how do I" questions wait in the Customer Care queue.

What it does
  • Looks up a signed-in customer's orders and explains the status in plain English
  • Raises cases for pickup or delivery, cancellations, returns and faults, linked to the order in Salesforce
  • Answers questions from JB's own knowledge articles
  • Checks a person is available, then hands the chat over
Who it helps

Customers get an answer straight away. Customer Care handles fewer routine contacts and receives a full conversation history on handoff.

Quote Assist Agent

Live in production

Turns a customer's email into a draft quote for a rep to check.

The problem it solves

Quote requests land in individual inboxes and get retyped by hand.

What it does
  • Reads the inbound email, or web form, and pulls out the account, PO and line items
  • Runs fraud checks, then drafts the quote in Salesforce
  • Flags anything it can't handle for the team to quote manually
  • A person reviews every quote before it goes out
Who it helps

Sales coordinators and reps check and sell instead of retyping. Customers get their quote sooner.

Quote-to-Order Agent

In user testing

Drafts the order from an approved quote and walks the orders team through each step.

The problem it solves

Once a quote is accepted, the same details are typed in again to create the order.

What it does
  • Drafts the order and saves it for review. It never submits on its own
  • Guides the team through JB's internal process notes
  • Pauses for finance holds, fraud and high-value flags, and quantity or stock limits
  • Starts with the highest-volume, lowest-complexity orders
Who it helps

The orders team spends time on exceptions rather than re-keying, with the safeguards built in.

Consumption guide: what's metered and what isn't

Most of this agreement is a per-user licence, with no usage meter. Usage is drawn down in two places: Flex Credits for agents that act on their own, and message credits for SMS, WhatsApp and email.

Unmetered

Covered by the per-user licence. Use it as much as your people need.

Metered

Usage draws down a pool, either Flex Credits or a prepaid message pack.

Fixed capacity

A set quantity is included, such as storage, logins or contacts. Not consumption, but there is a limit to size against.

Not usage-based

A service or support plan, with no meter.

How Flex Credits work

  • A standard agent action uses 20 Flex Credits. A voice action uses 30.
  • One interaction can use several actions, for example identifying a customer, finding their cases and adding a comment.
  • Prompts are measured in 2,000-token chunks.
  • The pool is shared across the whole organisation, not allocated per seat. Unused credits don't roll over past the end of the term.
  • Data 360 usage is metered separately.
  • Digital Wallet shows consumption as it happens.

Source: Salesforce Agentforce pricing and help documentation, October 2026. Multipliers are the published rate card.

Unmetered depends on how it is set up

In its standard form, unmetered use means a licensed person using an employee-facing feature themselves, in their own context. Three things decide whether that stays true:

  • Permissions. Salesforce documents that employee agent use is metered or unmetered depending on the permissions of the person using it. Each user needs the Unmetered User-Based AI licence and permission assigned. Without them, the same use draws Flex Credits.
  • Who starts it. Anything triggered by a flow, a scheduled job or an automation, with no person involved, is system-initiated and is metered.
  • How it is built. Custom-built agents and actions, and anything customers talk to directly, are metered per action.
  • Coworker. Unmetered Coworker needs the Unmetered User-Based AI licence and permission set assigned to each user, plus Coworker's own permission groups, and Data 360 activated. Salesforce notes that using Coworker also affects consumption of Data 360, conversations and Flex Credits.
Changing how a feature is configured, such as who it is assigned to, how it is triggered, or whether it is customised, can change whether it is metered. Usage in sandboxes and testing is metered too. Please check with your Salesforce team before making those changes, and keep Digital Wallet alerts switched on.
